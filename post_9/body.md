# Brevan Howard’s Star Boy: Minal Bathwal

Brevend Howard’s Minal Bathwal has discretely built one of the most phenomenal track records in modern macro investing. He has had no losing years since he started managing money during the 2008 financial crisis, an achievement made more striking by the structural shifts that have filled the markets over the past two decades. Some of his earliest and most significant gains came from trades in South Korean interest rates, where he positioned for both the direction and volatility of policy moves as the Bank of Korea responded aggressively to the global financial crisis. These Korean rate trades generated strong early returns, and also established his reputation within the firm as a trader capable of navigating emerging Asian markets. Based in Singapore at Brevan Howard Asset Management LLP, Bathwal has generated billions of dollars in profits by focusing on consistency rather than large flashy returns. Unlike several high profile hedge fund managers, his success has not come from bold trades, but more from a disciplined approach that is grounded in risk control.

A key element of Bathwal’s trading strategy is his focus on trade structure rather than pure market direction. His knowledge on derivatives and the understanding of global markets and trading skills he developed at Brevan Howard provided a strong background for Bathwal in the initial period of him managing money. He approaches macro ideas through the lens of payoffs, thereby creating positions that resemble options. For him losses are limited and thought of in advance, while the gains can be disproportionately large if the underlying thesis plays out. This careful approach allows him to invest in uncertain macro environments without requiring accurate timing. And in global macro, where being early is often indistinguishable from being wrong, this asymmetric construction has been extremely fundamental to his trading strategy. It enables him to remain positioned through volatility without exposing the fund to a potential catastrophic downside.

Furthermore Bathwal expresses his macro views less through single concentrated bets, rather he typically spreads one idea across multiple aspects. For example a view on monetary policy may be implemented through interest rate positions, yield curve trades, volatility exposure, FFX. By avoiding relying on singular bets, he reduces reliance on any single market behaving ideally. This allows gains in one leg to offset losses in another.

Another element of risk management Bathwal implements into his trading strategy is when the market moves against his positions, he is known to adjust or reduce exposure early, without emotional attachment to a trade. So beyond just strategy, his demeanor remains disciplined, which reflects his awareness of behavioural biases such as loss aversion and overconfidence that often lead traders to compound mistakes. So by avoiding the urge to “fight the market,” Bathwal preserves capital and he also maintains the flexibility to redeploy risk when conditions improve. This unemotional approach to losses has been a key aspect in avoiding large drawdowns over time.

Along with this emotionless approach, Bathwal often practices restraint and avoids excessive trading, even in volatile environments that tempt constant repositioning. By being selective with his trades and allowing positions time to develop, he limits transaction costs. This patience is then met with a balance between short-term tactical trades and medium term macro positions. These short term trades allow him to capitalize on current events and policy shifts, while the longer term positions reflect a structural outlook on rates, and regional macro dynamics. Together the allocation into these different time horizons smooth returns and reduce dependence on any single market regime.

Ultimately, Bathwal’s success illustrates that long term profitability in global macro is less about dramatic insight or risky trades and more about system design and structure. His approach is built to endure volatility and compound small advantages over time. His record emphasizes that consistency itself can be the most powerful edge.
